01 — Introduction

An operator's company, not a paper contractor.

EPROM was created to do one thing: take responsibility for someone else's plant and hand it back working better. That single discipline has grown into twelve service lines that touch the asset from the first flushing line to the last competency sign-off.

05 — Where we work

Assets we operate, maintain and certify.

The same twelve service lines are deployed across the whole hydrocarbon and power value chain — upstream gas plants through to the berth where the cargo leaves.

Oil refineries

Full O&M, turnarounds, catalyst handling, crude assay and product certification.

Gas plants — onshore & offshore

Upstream processing, pre-commissioning, start-up and global maintenance contracts.

Petrochemicals

Operation, rotating and static maintenance, integration studies with refining.

Fertilizer plants

Ammonia/urea plant operation, furnace and heater optimisation, reliability.

Terminals & tank farms

Stock reconciliation, vessel scheduling, demurrage, de-sludging, loading systems. See the full terminal scope ↓

Power stations

Operation and maintenance of electrical power generation assets.

Solar & renewables

Turn-key PV, off-grid, solar irrigation and street lighting; CSP for process heat.

Laboratories

Full laboratory outsourcing, ISO 17025 accreditation, LIMS, greenfield lab build.

06 — Credentials & track record

What is live, what is signed, what is being pursued.

We separate these deliberately. A client deserves to know the difference between an operating result and an ambition.

International position

Tier A — live operations abroad

Nigeria — Port Harcourt Refinery, Phase 1 (60,000 bpd), delivered by the EPROM–NETCO consortium: approximately 6.76 million barrels refined between December 2024 and May 2025. Plus pre-commissioning support at the Warri refinery. This is EPROM's flagship proof of international O&M capability.

Tier B — signed memoranda of understanding

Nine outside Egypt: NETCO (Nigeria) · UOP/Honeywell (USA) · Burgan One and EPICORP (Kuwait) · Dima Group and CIS (Iraq) · IFPT (France) · Fleet Energy (UAE) · Technip Energies (Italy) · NOC (Oman).

Tier C — active tenders & near-term pursuits

UAE — ADNOC 300 MMSCFD gas-field tender (with ENPPI) · Iraq — Karbala 140k O&M, Baghdad–Basra FCC, Gharraf gas · Jordan — Aqaba Port O&M.

Tier D — targeted markets

Priority tier 1: Oman, Kuwait, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Libya, Qatar. Tier 2: Nigeria, Tanzania, Algeria, Iraq, Kazakhstan, India, Thailand. Plus Angola registration and scope issued to Namibia, Romania, China and Senegal.

Delivery models

  • Full O&M — we run the plant against availability and cost KPIs.
  • Global maintenance — one integrated maintenance contract across several assets and sites.
  • Turnaround / call-off — mobilised scope for a shutdown window.
  • Technical services & secondment — certified specialists inside your organisation.
  • Consortium / joint venture — with a local or technology partner, as in Nigeria.
